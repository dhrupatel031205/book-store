About E-Granthalaya

E-Granthalaya is a digital bookstore project designed to provide an interactive and user-friendly experience for book lovers. Developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Flask , it offers a seamless platform for users to explore, browse, and manage books efficiently. The project integrates user authentication , allowing users to sign up, log in, and securely access their accounts. The book store section showcases a collection of books, where users can search, filter, and view book details. Additionally, the cart system enables users to add books they are interested in and manage their selections before proceeding with any actions. The database interaction is handled using XAMPP , ensuring that book details and user information are stored and retrieved efficiently. A well-structured contact us page allows users to submit inquiries or provide feedback, making communication easier. The about us page highlights the purpose and vision of E-Granthalaya, giving users insight into the platform. Session management ensures that users remain logged in until they decide to log out, enhancing security and convenience. The entire project follows a responsive design , ensuring a smooth experience across different devices, including desktops, tablets, and smartphones. With a focus on simplicity and functionality, E-Granthalaya aims to create a modern and efficient digital bookstore where users can engage with books effortlessly. It serves as a great platform for book enthusiasts looking for a streamlined and interactive reading experience.
